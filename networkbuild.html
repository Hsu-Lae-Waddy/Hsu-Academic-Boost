💡 Networking Learning Corner

📡

Modem

A modem connects your network to your Internet service provider.

📶

Router

A router forwards data between networks and can share an Internet connection.

🔀

Switch

A switch connects wired devices within a local network.

📶

Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi lets devices communicate wirelessly with a network access point.

🗄️

Server

A server provides services and resources to other devices on a network.

🔌

Ethernet

Ethernet is a common technology for wired network connections.
